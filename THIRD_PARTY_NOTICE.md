# Third-party content

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers the code of this project only.

## Cursor packs are not included

This repository ships **no cursor packs**. Animated cursor packs are the work of their
artists, and many of them (including the ones this app was developed with) state that
publication or duplication is prohibited. Download packs from the creators yourself,
keep them in your own cursors folder, and do not commit them to a fork.

`assets/bundled/` is an optional local folder for packs you have the rights to ship with
your own copy; its contents are ignored by git.
